*Four persona-specific, current-state journey maps for workshopr.io, one per persona in `PERSONAS.md` (Maya, Devin, Priya, Marcus). Method: Kalbach-style experience mapping. Each map covers six stages with goals, actions, touchpoints, thinking, an emotion score from -2 to +2, pain points, and opportunities.*

> ⚠️ **Research note:** Like the personas, these maps are **provisional** (reasoned, not yet validated by primary research). No interview transcripts or survey analysis behind them are on file. Emotion scores are interpretive rather than measured.

**Reading the maps:** stages run left to right; ★ marks a moment of truth, the point where the experience is won or lost. Feeling is scored -2 (worst) to +2 (best).

## Maya Okonkwo: one client engagement, end to end

**Scenario:** a paid half-day strategy workshop for a repeat-curious client. **Boundaries:** from the discovery call to the moment the next engagement is (or isn't) won.

| | 1 · Win the work | 2 · Design the agenda | 3 · Prep and rehearse | 4 · Deliver | 5 · Synthesize and send ★ | 6 · Follow up and re-win |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase** | Strategize | Plan | Plan | Facilitate | Synthesize | Post |
| **Goal** | Turn a maybe into a signed engagement | A credible, well-paced agenda without losing a weekend | Walk in knowing the plan holds | Run the room, not the tools | Client-ready recap out the same evening | Convert delivery into the next engagement |
| **Doing** | Discovery call; scopes the ask; writes the proposal | Assembles from the library; tunes timing; runs Agenda Check | Exports run-of-show; packs against materials list; rehearses openers | Runs timers, scripts and notes in Facilitator; photographs every flip chart | Drops board export and photos into Synthesize; edits themes into her voice; exports | Sends recap; waits; nudges; drafts the next SOW |
| **Touchpoints** | Zoom, email, her site | Planner, library, Agenda Check | Run-of-show export, print | Facilitator app, Sidekick | Synthesize, Markdown export, her template | Email, LinkedIn, the recap itself |
| **Thinking** | "Is this scope even facilitatable in half a day?" | "This used to take my whole Sunday." | "If the client re-orders modules tonight, I re-flow in minutes." | "Stay with the room; the laptop holds the plan." | "Every theme traces to a sticky. I can defend this." | "Did the workshop change anything for them?" |
| **Feeling** | Hopeful +1 | In craft +2 | Focused +1 | In flow +2 | Proud +1 | Uneasy -1 |
| **Pain points** | Unbillable pre-work; scope creep starts here | Little; polish is manual when client wants her brand | Late-night client changes; printing logistics | Energy dips she can feel but not measure | Pasting into her branded template is still manual | No evidence of impact; silence after send; repeat business rides on anecdotes |
| **Opportunities** | Proposal-ready agenda summary from a draft plan | Brandable agenda exports | Client-facing agenda share view | Post-hoc energy read from session events | **Branded one-click recap** (skip the template paste) | **Impact report + commitment tracking** |

**Emotional curve:** +1, +2, +1, +2, +1, -1. High through craft and delivery, then drops exactly where the product ends today: after send.

**Moments of truth:**
- ★ **The same-evening recap.** If it lands while the energy is warm, she looks exceptional; the whole product pays off here.
- ★ **"Where did this theme come from?"** Provenance answers the client challenge in seconds; without it, trust in the recap (and in her) wobbles.

**Top opportunities:** 1) Impact report and commitment tracking to close stage 6 (impact High, feasibility Mid). 2) Branded exports so stage 5 ends inside the product (High, High). 3) Client-facing agenda share for prep (Mid, High).

## Validation note

Emotion scores, the two-minute ceiling, the minute-12 freeze, and the seat-purchase dead end are all hypotheses. The research plan in `PERSONAS.md` (time-and-pain interviews, retro diary study, first-workshop interviews, buyer interviews, funnel data) doubles as the validation plan for these maps; run it before re-sequencing the roadmap on their basis.
