Research

8 jobs · the research set

  1. Find out where a research idea stands and what to do next

    Pick research-next /ux-os:research-next BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Reads the idea's folder and names the one thing to run now, so nothing gets skipped or done twice.

  2. Turn what I believe about a new idea into first models

    Pick research-discover /ux-os:research-discover BY NAMERuns when you type it

    One short interview about your beliefs, then proto personas, journeys and jobs, each marked as an assumption, and the belief to test first.

  3. Plan interviews

    Pick create-interview-script /ux-os:create-interview-script BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Asks about what people did, not what they would do, and ties every question to a belief it can confirm or break.

  4. Make personas from real interviews

    Pick create-personas /ux-os:create-personas BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Real mode needs five people from different organisations and links every line to the passage it came from.

    Rival design-research:user-persona writes a fluent persona whether or not anyone was interviewed. It is in the design-research pack.

  5. Map a journey

    Pick create-journey-map /ux-os:create-journey-map BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Current or future journey, with each step marked as belief or evidence.

    Rival design-research:journey-map does not separate what you believe from what you saw. It is in the design-research pack.

  6. Sort research notes into themes

    Pick affinity-diagram /ux-os:affinity-diagram BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Sorts from the bottom up, counts people rather than notes, and has a second agent re-sort blind to check the themes hold.

  7. Plan a usability test

    Pick create-usability-plan /ux-os:create-usability-plan BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Tasks, success measures and sample size, tied to the belief the test is meant to settle.

  8. Blueprint the service behind the screens

    Pick create-service-blueprint /ux-os:create-service-blueprint BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Seven lanes per step, checked by script, from research rather than from guesswork.

  6. Audit an existing design system

  2. Decide the page tree for a site

  3. Plan or restructure a public website

  4. Design navigation

  5. Set URL rules or plan redirects

    Pick url-structure /url-structure BY NAMERuns when you type it

Interaction design

7 jobs · the interaction design set

  1. Design a form

    Pick design-form /interaction-design:design-form ONLoads on its own

    Structure, chunking, validation, errors and completion in one run.

    Rival interaction-design:form-design is the first step of design-form, which then adds passes on choice load, memory, targets, errors, feedback and loading. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  2. Map a component's states

    Pick map-states /interaction-design:map-states ONLoads on its own

    States, events, guards and edge cases, then what each state looks like.

    Rival interaction-design:state-machine models the states but skips the loading, error and feedback passes map-states adds. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  3. Design what happens when something fails

    Pick error-flow /interaction-design:error-flow ONLoads on its own

    Prevention, detection, messaging and recovery.

    Rival interaction-design:error-handling-ux covers messages; error-flow also covers prevention and recovery paths. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  4. Design a first-run experience

    Pick design-onboarding /interaction-design:design-onboarding ONLoads on its own

    The activation path, what to reveal when, and time to first value.

    Rival interaction-design:onboarding-design is one step inside design-onboarding. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  5. Design the wait

    Pick loading-states /interaction-design:loading-states ONLoads on its own

    Spinners, skeletons, optimistic updates and progressive reveal.

    Rival feedback-patterns is for confirming an action, not for waiting on one.

  6. Tell people an action registered

    Pick feedback-patterns /interaction-design:feedback-patterns ONLoads on its own

    Confirmations, status changes and notifications.

    Rival loading-states is for waiting, not for confirming.

  7. Tune how one animation feels

    Pick animation-principles /interaction-design:animation-principles ONLoads on its own

    My most used MC Dean skill: easing, staging and follow-through for one specific motion.
