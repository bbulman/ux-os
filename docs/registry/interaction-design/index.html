Lost

  • Design a form: the pick is design-form, because interaction-design:form-design is the first step of design-form, which then adds passes on choice load, memory, targets, errors, feedback and loading.
  • Map a component's states: the pick is map-states, because interaction-design:state-machine models the states but skips the loading, error and feedback passes map-states adds.
  • Design what happens when something fails: the pick is error-flow, because interaction-design:error-handling-ux covers messages; error-flow also covers prevention and recovery paths.
  • Design a first-run experience: the pick is design-onboarding, because interaction-design:onboarding-design is one step inside design-onboarding.
