Testing

Final run, one case: 8 of 8 outcome checks with the skill, 4 of 8 without (13 of 13 with its own checks). 71s and $0.23 with, 33s and $0.11 without. Trigger test 2 of 2.

Build story

Written from scratch.

What the no-skill run missed. It stated invented cadences, roles and meetings as fact, claimed rules were enforced without reading CI or hooks, and left no exception route.

What the skill adds. ds-governance scan reads what really runs and says which commands can fail. check fails a decision with no single decider, a window that differs from the profile, and any standing body on a small team.

Known limit. It cannot see inside a program a gate runs, or whether a check is required on the host.
