## How it was built

Written on 9 November 2026 to replace nine single-law skills in MC Dean's interaction-design pack, as part of a set of eleven. [lawsofux.com](http://lawsofux.com) is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which forbids adaptations, so the site is credited for its list of thirty laws only, and every entry is written from the original research and later critiques in `references/evidence.md`. A checking script compared it with 43,321 existing skill files and the site's text: no five-word phrase or heading is shared with any design skill.

## What testing showed

The first hidden-case run refused to give even a rough bound from the numbers supplied, so the skill now states labelled ranges. The full results and every caveat are in the [repo readme](https://github.com/bbulman/interaction-design-skills). Across the six clean tests the new set tied the old pack on checks, 60 of 62 each. Blind judges ranked the new skill first on three and last on three. The final wording about length has not been tested.
