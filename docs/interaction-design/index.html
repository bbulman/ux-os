Interaction design

States, forms, feedback, errors, navigation patterns, UX laws and motion.

The happy path is the easy part. This set covers what happens on an error, during a load and when someone changes their mind.

What's missing: Blind judges split my eleven against MC Dean's pack three and three, and preferred plain Claude on the three longest jobs, because mine run long. So MC Dean's pack leads and mine run by name.

Jobs

What I hand to which skill in interaction design. All the jobs

  1. Design a form

    Pick design-form /interaction-design:design-form ONLoads on its own

    Structure, chunking, validation, errors and completion in one run.

    Rival interaction-design:form-design is the first step of design-form, which then adds passes on choice load, memory, targets, errors, feedback and loading. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  2. Map a component's states

    Pick map-states /interaction-design:map-states ONLoads on its own

    States, events, guards and edge cases, then what each state looks like.

    Rival interaction-design:state-machine models the states but skips the loading, error and feedback passes map-states adds. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  3. Design what happens when something fails

    Pick error-flow /interaction-design:error-flow ONLoads on its own

    Prevention, detection, messaging and recovery.

    Rival interaction-design:error-handling-ux covers messages; error-flow also covers prevention and recovery paths. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  4. Design a first-run experience

    Pick design-onboarding /interaction-design:design-onboarding ONLoads on its own

    The activation path, what to reveal when, and time to first value.

    Rival interaction-design:onboarding-design is one step inside design-onboarding. It is in the interaction-design pack.

  5. Design the wait

    Pick loading-states /interaction-design:loading-states ONLoads on its own

    Spinners, skeletons, optimistic updates and progressive reveal.

    Rival feedback-patterns is for confirming an action, not for waiting on one.

  6. Tell people an action registered

    Pick feedback-patterns /interaction-design:feedback-patterns ONLoads on its own

    Confirmations, status changes and notifications.

    Rival loading-states is for waiting, not for confirming.

  7. Tune how one animation feels

    Pick animation-principles /interaction-design:animation-principles ONLoads on its own

    My most used MC Dean skill: easing, staging and follow-through for one specific motion.
