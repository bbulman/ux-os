A JTBD mapping for workshopr.io (Christensen/Ulwick method): the core job people hire the platform for, its functional, emotional and social dimensions, the eight-stage job map, what gets hired today, underserved outcomes, and job statements per persona. Grounded in PRODUCT.md, PERSONAS.md and JOURNEYS.md.

⚠️ Research note: Provisional, like the personas (reasoned, not yet validated by primary research). No interview transcripts or survey analysis behind it are on file. The open questions in PERSONAS.md apply here too.

The core job

When I'm the one responsible for a workshop, I want to turn limited prep time into a session that runs well and outcomes that stick, so I can prove the room's time was well spent, and mine too.

Nobody wakes up wanting a facilitation platform. They wake up owning a room full of expensive people and a result they'll be judged on. The job is "make the workshop worth everyone's time, then prove it." This framing explains the whole persona set at once: Maya is judged by clients, Devin by his team, Priya by her own nerve, Marcus by a CFO. It also explains the north-star metric: a workshop only fully "happened" when it was designed, delivered and synthesized, because until the outcomes stick, the job isn't done.

The job map (Ulwick's eight stages)

StageWhat's happeningOutcome expectationWorkshopr todayUnderserved
1 · DefineFrame what the session must achieve, for whom, in how longBe certain a workshop is the right tool for the goalStrategist; goal field in PlannerThe strategy step is hidden; most users skip straight to the agenda
2 · LocateFind trusted building blocksMinimize time to an exercise that fits the time, group and energyLibrary (exercises, icebreakers, ready-made workshops)Fit signals are thin: what's safe for beginners, what works for this group size
3 · PrepareAssemble agenda, timings, materialsMinimize blank-page-to-credible-agenda timePlanner, ready-made workshops, materials listsBrand polish is manual; no client-facing share view
4 · ConfirmValidate the plan before the room doesMaximize confidence the plan survives contact with the roomAgenda CheckFirst-timer confidence framing; rehearsal support
5 · ExecuteRun the sessionStay present with the room, not the planFacilitator, Sidekick, Intervention cards, live layerCapture competes with facilitating; needs one-tap capture
6 · MonitorTrack time, energy, participationKnow it's drifting before it's lostTimers, live reactions, polls, help requestsEnergy is felt, not measured; no drift warning
7 · ModifyAdapt mid-flight, rescue stallsRecover without the room noticingRun-mode item controls; Intervention cardsNo re-flow math: what to cut to still end on time
8 · ConcludeSynthesize, recap, assign, follow upOutcomes leave the room and stickSynthesize, Workshop RecordThe big one: impact evidence, commitment tracking, integrations

The map makes the platform's shape legible: stages 2-5 are well served (that's the mature core), stages 1 and 8 are the frontier, and 6-7 are served for time but not for energy.
