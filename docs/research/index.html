Research

Interviews, surveys, usability tests, diary studies, card sorts, affinity diagrams, empathy maps, personas, journey maps, jobs to be done and research repositories.

Left alone, Claude will write you a persona from nothing. This set gives it a method for asking real people first.

What's missing: So far these have only run on made-up test data, and none has been compared with no skill at all.

How to run it

Install it once. You need Claude Code, and Node for the checking scripts.

/plugin marketplace add bbulman/ux-os
/plugin install ux-os@ux-os

Everything here is typed by name, and every name starts with /ux-os:. Not sure where to start? /ux-os:research-next says where an idea stands and the one thing to do now.

Personas, journeys, jobs and empathy maps each take a switch. proto builds from what you believe and labels it an assumption. real builds from your files, and every line links to the exact passage it came from. Real needs at least 5 people from different organisations, and stops if you don't have them.

Run /ux-os:init once per project and deliverables take your design system's fonts and colours. Design proposals then name a real component, or say none fits.

Example prompts

Swap volunteer-rota for your own idea, in two to four words.

  • I have an idea and no data yet.

    /ux-os:research-discover volunteer-rota

  • I want a quick persona from what I already believe.

    /ux-os:create-personas volunteer-rota proto

  • I've finished my interviews and want real personas.

    /ux-os:create-personas volunteer-rota real --evidence ./interviews/

  • New notes have come in.

    /ux-os:research-debrief volunteer-rota --evidence ./interviews/week-2/

  • I've talked to five organisations and want to stop guessing.

    /ux-os:research-synthesize volunteer-rota

  • I don't know what to do next.

    /ux-os:research-next volunteer-rota

  • I want deliverables to look like our design system.

    /ux-os:init

Jobs

What I hand to which skill in research. All the jobs

  1. Find out where a research idea stands and what to do next

    Pick research-next /ux-os:research-next BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Reads the idea's folder and names the one thing to run now, so nothing gets skipped or done twice.

  2. Turn what I believe about a new idea into first models

    Pick research-discover /ux-os:research-discover BY NAMERuns when you type it

    One short interview about your beliefs, then proto personas, journeys and jobs, each marked as an assumption, and the belief to test first.

  3. Plan interviews

    Pick create-interview-script /ux-os:create-interview-script BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Asks about what people did, not what they would do, and ties every question to a belief it can confirm or break.

  4. Make personas from real interviews

    Pick create-personas /ux-os:create-personas BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Real mode needs five people from different organisations and links every line to the passage it came from.

    Rival design-research:user-persona writes a fluent persona whether or not anyone was interviewed. It is in the design-research pack.

  5. Map a journey

    Pick create-journey-map /ux-os:create-journey-map BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Current or future journey, with each step marked as belief or evidence.

    Rival design-research:journey-map does not separate what you believe from what you saw. It is in the design-research pack.

  6. Sort research notes into themes

    Pick affinity-diagram /ux-os:affinity-diagram BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Sorts from the bottom up, counts people rather than notes, and has a second agent re-sort blind to check the themes hold.

  7. Plan a usability test

    Pick create-usability-plan /ux-os:create-usability-plan BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Tasks, success measures and sample size, tied to the belief the test is meant to settle.

  8. Blueprint the service behind the screens

    Pick create-service-blueprint /ux-os:create-service-blueprint BY NAMERuns when you type it

    Seven lanes per step, checked by script, from research rather than from guesswork.
