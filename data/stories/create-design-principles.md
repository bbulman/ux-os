## How it was built

Written on 9 November 2026, replacing `design-principles` in MC Dean's ux-strategy pack, as one of ten. The writer did not read the old skill, and worked from coverage notes in other words, a baseline run, and research listed in `references/evidence.md`. No five-word phrase or heading is shared with the skills it replaces.

## What testing showed

An independent agent wrote a sealed case for each skill. In round one this was one of the two where blind judges preferred the new skill to no skill. Each skill was then changed to write the finished thing first, with its working in a separate file, and on the rerun no skill came first here: the new sheet was tight, but it left the key finding in the working file and never said plainly to pull Quick Close off live emergency jobs. Full results are in the [repo readme](https://github.com/bbulman/strategy-skills).
