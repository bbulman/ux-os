## How it was built

Written on 9 November 2026 to replace `search-ux` in MC Dean's interaction-design pack, as part of a set of eleven. The writer did not read the old skill, and worked from a baseline run showing what the old skill missed, plus standards and research listed in `references/evidence.md`. A checking script compared it with 43,321 existing skill files: no five-word phrase or heading is shared with any design skill.

## What testing showed

The grading checks for this case were accidentally visible to the writer, so its score does not count as evidence. The full results and every caveat are in the [repo readme](https://github.com/bbulman/interaction-design-skills). An independent review found 25 problems across the set, all fixed. The final wording about length has not been tested.
