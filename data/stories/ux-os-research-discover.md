## How it was built

Written from scratch on 10 November 2026 in our own words. Chaining models into one discovery command comes from MC Dean's `discover` command (MIT), and the skill credits it. The difference is that everything here is labelled as belief, and the chain ends on what to test first instead of on the models.

The twelve skills start only when typed, so a chain reads each `SKILL.md` and follows it. The contract's Chains section holds the rules: one start, one interview of at most 12 questions, one finish, and a copy of anything it replaces.

## What testing showed

The empathy map is left out of the chain on judgement, because one moment adds few new beliefs to test. A planted scripted interview produced 15 beliefs and a "no view", and 14 of the 15 came back with no evidence from a small web search, which is the honest result for a made-up market.
