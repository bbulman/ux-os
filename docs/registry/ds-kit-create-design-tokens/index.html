Testing

Final run, one case: 8 of 9 outcome checks with the skill, 7 of 9 without (13 of 14 with its own checks). 151s and $0.37 with, 45s and $0.14 without. The one miss: a notice kept the same red in dark mode. Trigger test 2 of 2.

Build story

Written from scratch.

What the no-skill run missed. It renamed every old token with no alias, rewrote a consumer's runtime lookup, left dark-mode gaps as someone else's problem and wrote no DTCG file.

What the skill adds. ds-token-tiers fails aliases pointing the wrong way, value-named roles, modes that omit a role and consumers reading primitives. Old names stay as aliases with a ledger row.
